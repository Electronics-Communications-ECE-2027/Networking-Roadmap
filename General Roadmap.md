# 🌐 Computer Networking Roadmap

<p>
A structured roadmap for learning Computer Networking from fundamentals
to advanced network infrastructure, automation, security, and modern networking.
</p>

</div>
---
<div align="center">

## 🌐 Introduction

</div>

> **Computer Networking** is a fundamental field of computer and communication engineering concerned with how devices and systems communicate and exchange data. It forms the foundation of the **Internet, cloud computing, cybersecurity, IoT, distributed systems, and modern digital infrastructure**.
>
> This roadmap takes you from networking fundamentals and communication protocols to advanced concepts including **IPv4/IPv6, switching, VLANs, routing, OSPF, BGP, network security, Linux networking, automation, SDN, cloud networking, and modern network infrastructure**.
>
> Alongside theoretical concepts, the roadmap emphasizes **hands-on practice, network simulation, packet analysis, troubleshooting, and practical projects** to develop a strong understanding of how real-world networks are designed, configured, and operated.
